## Frequency-band teaching model

The source contrasts:

- **2.4 GHz:** longer range, better wall penetration, more congestion/interference.
- **5 GHz:** shorter range, faster/high-bandwidth use, less congestion, more affected by obstacles.
- **6 GHz:** more bandwidth and less congestion, but shorter range and more limited obstacle penetration.

## Channels

The source explicitly teaches:
- 2.4 GHz channels **1, 6, 11** as commonly used non-overlapping choices.
- 5 GHz channels beginning at **20 MHz** width.
- channel bonding to **40 / 80 / 160 MHz**.
- wider channels improve throughput but use more spectrum and may reduce effective range / increase sensitivity to obstacles and interference.
- regional regulations affect channel and power availability.
