## Display types
- LCD: liquid crystals control a separate backlight.
- LED monitor: in this source, generally an LED-backlit LCD.
- IPS: color accuracy and wide viewing angles.
- TN: fast response / high refresh; transcript `twisted pneumatic` corrected to **Twisted Nematic**.
- VA: stronger contrast and deeper blacks than IPS/TN, per source.
- Mini-LED: still LCD; thousands of small LEDs and improved local dimming.
- OLED: per-pixel light, true black, high contrast, thin/flexible; source-stated burn-in risk.

## Display components
- Touchscreen: direct touch interaction.
- Digitizer: detects touch position and converts motion into digital signals.
- Display panel: produces image.
- Inverter: older CCFL systems; low-voltage DC → high-voltage AC.
- Flashlight test: faint image suggests panel is producing an image while backlight is not functioning.

### Pixel density

### Refresh rate

### Color gamut
- range of colors the display can produce
