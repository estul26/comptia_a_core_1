# Objective 3.8 — Deep Translation Review

## Source structure
Objective 3.8 covers four printer types:

1. Inkjet
2. Laser
3. Thermal
4. Impact

## Inkjet
Source components:
- ink cartridge
- detached or cartridge-integrated printhead
- pickup roller
- feed rollers
- carriage belt

Source calibration:
- test page
- alignment patterns / color gradients
- automatic or manual
- especially after ink-cartridge replacement or maintenance

Source maintenance:
- clean clogged printheads
- built-in cleaning utility or printhead cleaning kit
- replace depleted ink
- follow manufacturer cartridge guidance
- carefully clear jams and remaining paper debris

## Laser

### Seven-stage imaging process
1. Processing
2. Charging
3. Exposing
4. Developing
5. Transferring
6. Fusing
7. Cleaning

Source components/concepts:
- onboard printer memory
- photosensitive/imaging drum
- laser beam
- toner
- transfer belt/roller
- fuser assembly / heated rollers
- cleaning blade/brush

Source maintenance:
- replace toner
- maintenance kit
- source examples: fuser, transfer roller, cleaning blade, other wear parts
- reset maintenance/page counters after maintenance if used by printer
- clean dust/toner/debris
- source recommends lint-free cloth/swab dampened with isopropyl alcohol while following manufacturer guidance
- calibrate periodically, especially after toner replacement/maintenance

The transfer-stage transcript contains a garbled analogy (`a stab transferring ink`). The reviewed translation omits the broken analogy and preserves the actual mechanism.

## Thermal
Source:
- heat, not ink/toner
- specially coated thermal paper
- feed assembly
- heating element
- paper sensitivity varies
- temperature should follow manufacturer guidance
- higher-sensitivity paper needs less heat but may fade more
- store paper cool/dry/away from direct sunlight
- heat/humidity can degrade paper

Maintenance:
- replace depleted paper rolls
- clean heating element
- remove internal debris

## Impact
Source:
- mechanical printhead strikes ink ribbon
- tractor feed moves/aligns paper
- continuous-form paper
- multipart/carbon-copy forms
- business examples: invoices, service tickets, contracts

Maintenance:
- replace worn ribbon
- replace worn printhead when necessary
- keep/replenish continuous paper stock

The opening impact paragraph contains garbled `card and paper` wording. The reviewed lesson uses only the clearly supported multipart-form and later carbon-copy content.

## Translation approach
- Preserve printer-specific maintenance rather than merging consumables/components.
- Preserve all source-stated process stages and procedures.
- Omit broken analogies instead of guessing.
- Do not import maintenance intervals, parts or procedures absent from Objective 3.8.
